# Frontend Design

## Brand direction
EURO FINISHES is a premium decorative wall finishes brand. The visual language should feel architectural, editorial, warm, restrained, and confident—not like a generic painting contractor template.

## Brand palette
Use these as the current proposed digital palette:
- Warm ivory: #F5F1E9
- Graphite: #292A2C
- Champagne stone: #B7A58B
- Travertine: #DDD3C4
- Olive grey: #77766E
- Terracotta accent: #8C4939
- White: #FFFFFF

Treat these as provisional design tokens, not verified physical paint specifications. Maintain readable contrast and use terracotta sparingly.

## Design principles
- Use generous whitespace and clear visual hierarchy.
- Pair an editorial display serif with a restrained sans-serif for interface text.
- Use large architectural imagery and authentic close-up finish textures.
- Prefer strong composition, grid discipline, and subtle borders over excessive cards and shadows.
- Keep buttons, links, focus states, and form controls consistent.
- Use restrained hover and reveal effects.
- Avoid generic gradients, glassmorphism, neon, decorative clutter, and excessive rounded corners.
- Do not invent company claims, certifications, years of experience, product specifications, customer testimonials, or project statistics.

## Logo integrity
- Treat the supplied EURO FINISHES logo as the source of truth.
- Preserve its proportions, wordmark layout, globe artwork, and tagline.
- Never stretch, skew, recolour, or recreate the logo inaccurately.
- If a transparent/vector asset is needed, request or prepare a faithful conversion and compare it against the supplied source.
- Ensure the globe used in the preloader aligns with the actual O artwork.

## Responsive design
- Design mobile, tablet, and desktop deliberately rather than scaling desktop blindly.
- Prevent horizontal overflow and text collisions.
- Keep logo proportions intact.
- Make navigation and controls touch-friendly.
- Use responsive image crops that preserve the architectural subject.

## Accessibility
- Use semantic HTML and meaningful heading order.
- Provide visible keyboard focus.
- Maintain sufficient colour contrast.
- Supply useful alt text for meaningful imagery.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Do not communicate essential information through colour alone.

## Review checklist
Before considering a screen complete, inspect it at common desktop and mobile widths. Check typography, alignment, spacing, image quality, contrast, responsive behaviour, empty/loading/error states, and consistency with the brand.
